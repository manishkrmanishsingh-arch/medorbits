FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are hostel charges included?

Usually no. Hostel, mess and other expenses are charged separately.


Can fees change every year?

Yes. Universities and counselling authorities may revise fees for every academic session.


Can I pay fees in installments?

It depends upon the institution. Contact the respective college for payment policies.
