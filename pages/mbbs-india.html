Main Consideration Eligibility & Proof Planning Focus Official Verification ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENTS General Requirements for MBBS Admission Exact criteria vary by admission year, category, counselling authority, institution, and state. ✅ General Eligibility Checklist • Required qualifying examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English. • NEET-UG qualification as prescribed for the relevant admission cycle. • Applicable minimum age, academic, and category conditions. • Domicile or other eligibility requirements where relevant. • Compliance with counselling and institutional rules. 🗂️ Common Document Checklist • Class 10 and Class 12 certificates and marksheets. • NEET admit card, scorecard, and counselling documents. • Identity proof, photographs, and signature files. • Domicile, category, EWS, PwD, or NRI documents where applicable. • Transfer, migration, medical fitness, allotment, and reporting records. Seat matrix, cutoffs, category rules, counselling schedules, and fee structures can change. Always verify the latest official notification before applying.
