An MBA is a postgraduate management degree that develops leadership, analytical, communication, financial, strategic, and decision-making skills for business and organisational roles. 📊 Business Knowledge Study finance, marketing, operations, strategy, economics, analytics, organisational behaviour, and management. 🧠 Leadership Skills Develop decision-making, communication, negotiation, team management, and strategic thinking. 🚀 Career Mobility Move into managerial, consulting, entrepreneurial, product, finance, marketing, or operations roles. Global Trade Strategy ELIGIBILITY General MBA Admission Requirements Eligibility varies by institution, entrance exam, work experience requirement, category, and admission year. 🎓 Bachelor's Degree Applicants generally need a recognised undergraduate degree with the minimum marks prescribed by the institution. 📝 Entrance Examination Admission may depend on CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, GMAT, university tests, or merit-based selection. 💼 Work Experience Some programs prefer or require work experience, while many regular MBA programs accept fresh graduates. Entrance criteria, application deadlines, interview processes, and cutoffs change by institution. Always verify the latest official admission notification. ENTRANCE EXAMS Common MBA Admission Tests CAT Widely used for admission to IIMs and many leading management institutes in India. XAT Accepted by XLRI and several participating business schools. MAT / CMAT Used by many management institutes for MBA and PGDM admissions. GMAT Commonly considered for international MBA programs and selected Indian institutions.
