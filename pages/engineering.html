Engineering programs combine mathematics, science, design, technology, practical training, and problem-solving. Students select a branch according to their interests, strengths, and long-term career plans. 🧠 Problem Solving Apply mathematical and scientific principles to design systems, products, software, infrastructure, and industrial solutions. 🛠️ Technical Skills Develop practical knowledge through laboratories, projects, internships, coding, design, simulation, and fieldwork. 🚀 Career Flexibility Build careers in technology, manufacturing, construction, energy, electronics, consulting, startups, research, or management. AI ML Analytics ELIGIBILITY General Engineering Admission Requirements Eligibility varies by college, exam, state, category, and admission year. 📘 Academic Qualification Applicants generally require the prescribed qualifying examination with Physics, Mathematics, and other applicable subjects. 📝 Entrance Examination Admission may depend on JEE Main, JEE Advanced, state entrance tests, university exams, or merit-based routes. 📄 Documents Academic certificates, entrance scorecards, identity proof, photographs, domicile, category, counselling, and reporting documents. Entrance rules, minimum marks, counselling procedures, and seat categories may change. Check the latest official information for the relevant admission cycle. ENTRANCE EXAMS Common Engineering Admission Routes JEE Main Common national-level route for NITs, IIITs, and several participating institutions. JEE Advanced Admission route for IIT programs for eligible candidates who meet current requirements. State CETs State-level examinations used for admission to participating engineering institutions. University Exams Independent entrance examinations conducted by selected private or deemed universities.
