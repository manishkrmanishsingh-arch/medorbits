Key Consideration Recognition & Faculty Support Application Review ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENTS General Requirements for Indian Students Exact eligibility varies by country, university, admission year, and applicable regulations. ✅ General Eligibility Checklist • Required qualifying examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology, and English. • NEET qualification where applicable for Indian medical aspirants. • Minimum age and academic criteria prescribed for the admission year. • Valid passport and medical fitness. • Compliance with university and regulatory requirements. 🗂️ Common Document Checklist • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates. • NEET scorecard and related documents where required. • Passport, identity proof, and photographs. • Medical fitness, HIV or health reports if requested. • Admission letter, visa forms, affidavits, and financial documents. Recognition rules, licensing pathways, and eligibility requirements can change. Students should verify current official regulations before selecting a university.
