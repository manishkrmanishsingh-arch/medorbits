Frequently Asked Questions

Answers Before You Contact Us

Some of the most common questions asked by students, parents and job seekers.

How do I apply through MedOrbit?

Simply contact us through WhatsApp, phone or the enquiry form. Our counsellor will guide you according to your qualification, budget and career goals.

Do you provide MBBS admission guidance?

Yes. Guidance is available for India and selected international destinations depending upon eligibility and admission criteria.

Do you help with Study Abroad?

Yes. We assist with country selection, university selection, documentation and admission support.

Can I visit your office directly?

Yes. However, prior appointment is recommended for faster service.

Do you also provide Job Assistance?

Yes. We provide career guidance and employment assistance across multiple sectors depending upon eligibility and employer requirements.

Does MedOrbit guarantee admission or jobs?

No. Admissions and employment depend upon official eligibility, availability, documentation and the respective institution or employer.
