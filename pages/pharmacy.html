Why Choose Pharmacy?

Pharmacy is one of the fastest-growing healthcare professions. Pharmacists play an essential role in drug development, patient care, hospitals, pharmaceutical industries, clinical research, regulatory affairs, and healthcare management.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Mathematics.

  • State or university entrance examination wherever applicable.
  • Valid documents including mark sheets and identity proof.

Career Scope

Career Opportunities After Pharmacy

Pharmacy graduates can work in hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, clinical research organisations, laboratories, regulatory bodies, retail pharmacies and healthcare businesses.

💊

Community Pharmacist

Dispense medicines, guide patients and manage retail or community pharmacy operations.

🏥

Hospital Pharmacist

Support doctors and patients with medicine management, dosage review and hospital pharmacy services.

🧪

Quality Control Analyst

Test pharmaceutical products and ensure that medicines meet prescribed quality and safety standards.

🔬

Research Associate

Assist in medicine development, formulation research, laboratory studies and scientific documentation.

📋

Regulatory Affairs Executive

Prepare submissions and help pharmaceutical products comply with applicable regulatory requirements.

🩺

Clinical Research Associate

Coordinate clinical studies, review study documentation and support research compliance.

🏭

Production Executive

Supervise pharmaceutical manufacturing, production records, safety systems and operational quality.

✍️

Medical Writer

Develop scientific, clinical, regulatory and educational content for healthcare organisations.

📈

Pharmaceutical Marketing

Work in product management, institutional sales, healthcare marketing and business development.

🎓

Teaching and Academics

Pursue postgraduate study and work in pharmacy education, academic research or institutional training.

Many Pharmacy programmes accept students who studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Eligibility must be checked against the specific college and admission rules.
