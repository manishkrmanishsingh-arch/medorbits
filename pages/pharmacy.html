Focus Compliance & Trials Career Research / Regulatory ELIGIBILITY General Pharmacy Admission Requirements Exact eligibility varies by program, institution, state, category, and admission year. 📘 Academic Qualification Applicants generally need the prescribed qualifying examination and science subjects required for the selected pharmacy program. 📝 Entrance or Merit Route Admission may be based on state entrance tests, university exams, counselling, or qualifying examination marks. 📄 Documents Academic certificates, identity proof, photographs, domicile, category, entrance score, and counselling documents. Eligibility, approvals, seat categories, registration rules, and application dates can change. Verify the latest official notification and institutional status before admission. ADMISSION ROUTES Common Pharmacy Selection Methods State Entrance Exams Used by several states for pharmacy admissions and centralised counselling. University Entrance Tests Conducted by selected universities and private institutions. Merit-Based Admission Based on qualifying examination marks where permitted by the institution. Postgraduate Exams Applicable entrance scores may be required for selected M.Pharm or advanced programs.
