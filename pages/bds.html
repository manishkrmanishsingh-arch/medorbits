BDS is the main undergraduate professional qualification in dentistry. It combines basic medical sciences, dental subjects, laboratory work and supervised clinical training. 🦷 What You Study • Human anatomy, physiology and biochemistry • Dental anatomy and oral pathology • Pharmacology, microbiology and dental materials • Oral surgery, orthodontics and prosthodontics • Clinical diagnosis and patient management 🎯 Who Should Choose BDS? • Students interested in healthcare and oral medicine • Those with patience, precision and communication skills • Aspirants seeking a recognised professional degree • Students interested in clinical practice or specialisation • Those comfortable with academic and clinical training ELIGIBILITY General BDS Admission Requirements Exact rules differ by year, category, state and institution. Always verify the current official counselling notification. 📘 Academic Qualification Required qualifying examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Biotechnology and English as applicable. 📝 NEET-UG BDS admission in India is generally based on NEET-UG qualification and participation in counselling. 📄 Documents Academic certificates, NEET documents, identity proof, photographs, domicile/category documents and counselling forms.
