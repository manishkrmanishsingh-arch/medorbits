# 🌍 About MedOrbit Foundation

MedOrbit Foundation is an educational and career guidance organization that provides professional consultancy services across multiple sectors.

Our mission is to simplify admissions, career planning, employment opportunities, skill development, and higher education through transparent guidance and technology.

The platform serves students, fresh graduates, working professionals, and institutions by offering reliable counselling and end-to-end support.

---

# 🎯 Our Vision

To become India's most trusted education and career guidance platform by connecting students and professionals with genuine opportunities.
