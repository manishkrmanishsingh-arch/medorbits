MEDICAL ADMISSION MBBS admission in India MBBS admission generally involves NEET UG qualification followed by the appropriate counselling process. Seat categories and procedures vary by institution and jurisdiction. All India counselling Centralised counselling for eligible seats as notified by the competent authority. State counselling State-level counselling based on domicile, eligibility and applicable rules. Deemed universities Participate through the officially designated counselling process. Private colleges Fees, bonds and seat categories differ by state and institution. NRI seats Require additional eligibility and relationship or sponsorship documents. Reporting Complete physical or online reporting within the allotted deadline.
