MedOrbit helps students organise the process clearly. ✅ General Eligibility Checklist • Required qualifying examination with applicable science subjects. • Minimum age and academic criteria prescribed for the chosen admission route. • Valid NEET qualification where applicable for Indian medical aspirants. • Passport availability for international admission applications. • Medical fitness and other university-specific eligibility requirements. • Compliance with current regulatory and counselling guidelines. 🗂️ Common Document Checklist • Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates. • NEET scorecard, admit card, and application details where required. • Government-issued identity proof and recent passport-size photographs. • Passport and supporting travel documents for overseas admission. • Transfer, migration, domicile, category, or gap documents as applicable. • Medical fitness, affidavit, undertaking, and institutional forms if requested.
