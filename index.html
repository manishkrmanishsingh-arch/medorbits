Career & Employment Assistance

Connecting Eligible Candidates with Relevant Opportunities

In addition to education and admission guidance, MedOrbit provides career-support services intended to help eligible candidates discover and prepare for suitable opportunities shared by participating employers, recruitment agencies and authorised organisations.

Assistance may include profile evaluation, opportunity information, interview preparation, documentation guidance and general support during legitimate recruitment processes.

Recruitment may occur through scheduled interviews, walk-in drives, recruitment programmes, employer referrals, apprenticeships, internships, contractual appointments or other employer-managed processes.

Does MedOrbit provide career and employment support?
